How RSVP Reading Works

One fixed reading point, adjustable pace, and fewer chances to lose your place.

1

Import Your Reading

Load books, documents, websites, articles, or scanned pages directly into Absorb. Your reading content and progress are designed to stay on your device.

2

Focus on the Pivot

Words are displayed one by one. The red letter is the Optimal Recognition Point (ORP). Keep your eyes fixed there.

3

Stop Subvocalizing

By flashing words faster than you can pronounce them in your head, Absorb helps you process text visually and focus deeply.

What is Absorb?
An iPhone RSVP reader that displays text one word at a time with adjustable speed and ORP highlighting.
What can I import?
EPUB, PDF, TXT, websites, articles, and camera-scanned pages, especially when the text extracts cleanly.
Which languages?
Books and reading content are available in English, Polish, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Hungarian, Swahili, and Czech.
Is it private?
Absorb is local-first, with imported books, website text, scans, and progress designed to stay on device.
Is it Spritz-style?
Yes. Absorb uses one-word-at-a-time RSVP pacing with a highlighted pivot letter.

For reading when pages feel noisy.

Absorb keeps the word stream in one place, so your eyes are not constantly tracking lines, margins, and page jumps.

  • Fixed gaze: Keep your eyes near the highlighted pivot instead of scanning across the page.
  • Adjustable pace: Slow down for dense passages and speed up when the text feels easy.
  • Reading history: Track progress, goals, words read, and average WPM across your library.
