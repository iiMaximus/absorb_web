How RSVP Reading Works

A simple technique to unlock your brain's natural reading speed.

1

Import Your Books

Load any EPUB directly into Absorb. Everything stays on your device for complete privacy and lightning-fast loading.

2

Focus on the Pivot

Words are displayed one by one. The red letter is the Optimal Recognition Point (ORP). Keep your eyes fixed there.

3

Stop Subvocalizing

By flashing words faster than you can pronounce them in your head, Absorb helps you process text visually and focus deeply.

Perfect for ADHD and focus issues.

Traditional reading requires constant eye movement across lines and pages, which can easily lead to distraction and lost place. RSVP reading eliminates this completely.

  • Zero Eye Tracking: Your eyes stay locked in one place, significantly reducing cognitive load.
  • Flow State: The constant stream of words pulls you into deep, uninterrupted focus.
  • Customizable Speeds: Start comfortably at 250 WPM and smoothly scale up to 600, 800, or even 1000 WPM as you train.
