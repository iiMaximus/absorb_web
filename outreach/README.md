# Absorb AI Visibility Outreach Kit

This kit turns the on-site SEO/GEO work into off-site mentions, backlinks, and AI-corroboration signals.

## Goal

Make Absorb appear across the kinds of sources AI systems and search engines use for app recommendations:

- App directories and launch platforms
- iOS productivity roundups
- Speed reading, reading, and ADHD/focus blogs
- YouTube demos and transcripts
- Newsletters for indie apps, students, productivity, and quantified self
- Thoughtful Reddit/Hacker News/Product Hunt discussions where self-promotion is allowed
- Podcast notes and creator pages

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

1. Publish or update one useful guide page.
2. Pitch five relevant newsletters/blogs/directories.
3. Post one native demo video with a transcript.
4. Answer three real community questions without copy-pasting sales language.
5. Check Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, AI prompts, and referral logs.

## Measurement

Track:

- Referring domains and brand mentions
- Google/Bing indexed pages
- Bing AI Performance citations and grounding queries
- Manual prompts in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, and Google AI Mode
- App Store product page clicks and installs
