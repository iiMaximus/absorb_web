# Video Demo Script

## 30-Second Demo

Hook:
"If you lose your place while reading on your phone, this is a different way to read."

Demo:
"This is Absorb. Instead of showing a whole page, it displays one word at a time. You set the words-per-minute pace, keep your eyes on the highlighted pivot letter, and read without scanning across lines."

Feature beats:
- Import EPUB, PDF, TXT, websites, articles, or camera scans
- Read books and text in many languages
- Read with RSVP pacing
- Use optimal recognition point highlighting
- Track reading progress and WPM
- Keep imported reading content local-first

Close:
"Absorb is on the App Store. Search Read Fast Absorb or visit the link in the description."

Description:
Absorb is an iPhone RSVP speed reading app for focused reading sessions. It supports EPUB, PDF, TXT, website, article, and camera scan import, includes books in many languages, highlights the optimal recognition point, and is designed around local-first reading privacy.
