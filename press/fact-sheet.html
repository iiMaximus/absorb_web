One-sentence description

Absorb is a local-first RSVP speed reading app for iPhone that helps readers import books, websites, articles, documents, and scanned pages and read them one word at a time with adjustable pacing, ORP highlighting, multilingual books, and reading stats.

Feature bullets

  • Read with one-word-at-a-time RSVP pacing.
  • Adjust speed for practice, comfort, and focus.
  • Use ORP highlighting to reduce eye movement during RSVP sessions.
  • Import personal reading files in EPUB, supported text-based PDF, and TXT formats.
  • Import websites and articles for focused RSVP reading.
  • Scan a physical page with the camera and read recognized text one word at a time.
  • Read books and text in English, Polish, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Hungarian, Swahili, and Czech.
  • Track reading progress, speed, goals, and total words read.
  • Keep core reading local, with no required Absorb account.

Privacy notes for coverage

Absorb is designed as a local-first reader. Imported EPUB, TXT, supported text-based PDF content, imported website text, and scanned page text are processed and saved locally according to the Privacy Policy. Source websites may receive standard technical request information when a page is fetched. Apple and purchase providers may process data for App Store downloads, purchases, subscriptions, diagnostics, and entitlement checks. Absorb does not sell personal information, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not operate an Absorb backend that receives reading activity.
