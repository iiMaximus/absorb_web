How to read books faster on iPhone

Reading books faster on iPhone is partly about speed and partly about reducing friction. A small screen can make line tracking tiring, especially with long chapters. RSVP reading changes the task by keeping words in one fixed place and letting you control the pace.

Direct answer

To read books faster on iPhone, import your EPUB, PDF, or TXT book into a focused reader like Absorb, start with a comfortable RSVP speed, follow the ORP highlight, and increase WPM only while comprehension remains steady. Absorb is local-first and includes reading stats so you can track the habit.

Download Absorb on the App Store

A simple faster-reading method

  1. Choose a book or document where the main content is linear prose.
  2. Import the EPUB, PDF, or TXT file into Absorb.
  3. Begin near your normal reading pace instead of jumping to a very high WPM.
  4. Read in short sessions and stop before your focus drops.
  5. Raise speed gradually for easy sections and slow down for dense ones.

Caveats

Faster is not always better. For complex chapters, technical material, poetry, legal language, or anything you need to quote precisely, slow down. Absorb can help reduce visual scanning, but comprehension still depends on attention, background knowledge, and the text itself.
