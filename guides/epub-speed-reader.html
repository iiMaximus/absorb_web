EPUB speed reader for iPhone

An EPUB speed reader turns ebook text into a paced reading experience. Instead of scanning lines on a page, an RSVP reader displays one word or short phrase at a time so you can keep your eyes in one place and move through chapters at a chosen speed.

Direct answer

Absorb is an iOS EPUB speed reader for people who want to import ebooks and read them with RSVP pacing. It supports EPUB import, optimal recognition point highlighting, reading stats, and local-first handling of imported content.

Download Absorb on the App Store

Why EPUB works well with RSVP

EPUB files are usually text-first, which makes them a natural fit for one-word-at-a-time reading. Fiction, nonfiction, essays, and narrative chapters often translate better to RSVP than documents with complex tables or heavy page layout.

How to read an EPUB faster in Absorb

  1. Import your EPUB file into Absorb on iPhone.
  2. Start at a speed where comprehension feels easy.
  3. Keep your gaze near the highlighted ORP letter.
  4. Increase WPM gradually for familiar or lighter material.
  5. Pause or slow down for names, dense ideas, quoted passages, or technical sections.

Best EPUB content for speed reading

Content type Fit for RSVP Note
Novels and memoirs Strong Linear prose usually works well.
Business and self-improvement books Strong Use slower speeds for lists and frameworks.
Textbooks Mixed Pause for diagrams, equations, and review sections.
Reference books Limited Jumping around may be more important than pace.

Caveats

EPUB speed reading is not the same as guaranteed comprehension at high speeds. Treat WPM as a control, not a score. If a chapter matters, slow down and reread. Absorb helps with focused pacing, but it does not replace active study or note-taking.
