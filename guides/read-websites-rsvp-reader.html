Website import guide

Read websites with an RSVP reader on iPhone

Long articles can be tiring on a phone because the page keeps asking your eyes to track lines, ads, margins, and scroll jumps. Absorb can turn imported website or article text into a calmer RSVP reading session.

Direct answer

Absorb lets iPhone readers import websites and articles, then read the extracted text one word at a time with adjustable RSVP pacing and optimal recognition point highlighting. It works best with text-heavy pages that can be extracted cleanly.

Download Absorb on the App Store

How website import helps

Instead of staying inside a busy web page, you can move article text into a fixed reading point. Absorb keeps the word stream in one place, lets you set a comfortable WPM, and preserves the core RSVP experience used for books and documents.

Best website content for RSVP

Web content Fit for RSVP Note
Articles and essays Strong Linear prose usually imports and reads well.
Documentation pages Mixed Slow down for code, tables, steps, and reference sections.
Paywalled or login-only pages Limited Access and extraction may not work if the page blocks import.
Interactive dashboards Limited Layout-heavy or app-like pages are not ideal for RSVP reading.

Privacy and website caveats

Absorb is designed as a local-first reader, but importing a website still involves the source site. The website you import may receive standard technical request information, such as IP address and user agent, when the page is fetched. After text is imported, Absorb is designed to handle the reading experience on device according to the Privacy Policy.
