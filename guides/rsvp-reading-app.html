RSVP reading guide

What is an RSVP reading app?

An RSVP reading app displays text one word or short phrase at a time in a fixed position. RSVP stands for rapid serial visual presentation. The goal is to reduce page scanning, keep your eyes still, and help you move through text at a controlled words-per-minute pace.

Direct answer

Absorb is an iOS RSVP reading app for people who want focused, one-word-at-a-time reading. It lets you import EPUB, PDF, and TXT files, read locally on your device, adjust speed, and use optimal recognition point highlighting to keep your eyes anchored.

How RSVP reading works

Traditional reading asks your eyes to travel across lines, jump to the next line, and repeatedly find your place. RSVP removes most of that movement by putting each word in the same visual area. You set the speed, press play, and let the app advance the text.

In Absorb, the highlighted letter marks the word's optimal recognition point, sometimes called the pivot. Keeping your gaze near that point can make the stream feel steadier because every word is visually aligned around a recognizable center.

When RSVP is useful

  • Previewing articles, documents, or chapters when you want momentum.
  • Reading on a phone without constant line tracking.
  • Practicing faster reading speeds in a controlled way.
  • Reducing the tendency to lose your place on dense pages.

RSVP reading compared with normal reading

Reading mode Best for Tradeoff
RSVP Focus, speed practice, short sessions, phone reading Less natural for skimming diagrams, tables, footnotes, or jumping around a page
Page reading Deep study, annotation, references, visual layout Requires more eye movement and place-keeping
Audio Hands-free listening Harder to inspect spelling, formulas, or exact wording

How to start with RSVP in Absorb

  1. Import an EPUB, PDF, or TXT file into Absorb on your iPhone.
  2. Start near your comfortable reading speed, such as 250 to 300 WPM.
  3. Keep your eyes fixed near the highlighted pivot letter.
  4. Increase speed gradually only when comprehension still feels steady.
  5. Pause for difficult paragraphs, names, code, formulas, or anything you want to reread.

Important facts

  • RSVP can help with pace and focus, but reading speed and comprehension vary by person and material.
  • Absorb is a reading and productivity tool, not a medical or educational diagnosis tool.
  • Imported reading content is designed to stay local to your device according to Absorb's privacy policy.
