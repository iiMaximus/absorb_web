Questions before you tap download

Keep the path simple.

Is Absorb only for speed reading?
No. Speed is one part of it. The bigger win is focus: keeping the text in one place so your attention has fewer chances to slip.
Will I understand everything at high speed?
No app should promise that. Start slower for dense books, raise the pace for easier passages, and pause whenever you need to think.
What can I read in Absorb?
Use the public-domain catalog or import EPUB, TXT, pasted text, and text-based PDFs. Scanned or protected PDFs may not extract cleanly.
Is my reading private?
Absorb is local-first. Imported reading content, progress, and preferences are designed to stay on your device without an Absorb account.
